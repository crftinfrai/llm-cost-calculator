TRANSPARENT METHODOLOGY

Why cost per successful task matters

A low token price does not always mean a low cost for a completed outcome. This calculator applies the selected success rate to estimate the effective number of attempts, then reports both the cost of an attempt and the cost of a successful task.

Monthly cost=cost per attempt × tasks ÷ success rate

Prompt caching is blended between regular input pricing and the provider's cache-read pricing. Add tool costs separately when your workflow uses search, code execution, image generation, speech, or other billable services.
